FAQ

Budgeting without a bank: your questions

Can I budget without connecting my bank?

Yes. In Pocklune you enter your income, bills and spending, or import a statement you choose. The app never asks for your bank login and uses no aggregator.

Why budget without a bank connection?

No third-party service gets access to your accounts, you stay in control of what goes into your budget, and nothing is added without your confirmation.

Where is my data stored?

Your amounts, receipts and photos stay on your phone. The account is only used for sign-in and to manage your subscriptions.

How much does Pocklune cost?

The core budget is free. Plus and Premium are optional. The price is shown on the App Store or Google Play before any payment.

Does Pocklune show ads?

No. There are no ads and no ad trackers in the app, and your data is never sold.

How does Pocklune work out what I have left?

It starts from your monthly income, subtracts your bills and the spending you've entered, and shows what's left to spend. Tap any amount to see where it comes from.

Which phones does Pocklune run on?

iPhone (App Store) and Android (Google Play).

Can I cancel a subscription easily?

Yes. Subscriptions renew automatically and can be cancelled anytime from your Apple or Google Play account.
