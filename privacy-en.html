Purposes and legal bases (GDPR)

  • Providing the local budget, account, sign-in and subscription entitlements: performance of contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Answering support requests and providing Premium AI at your request: performance of contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Securing the service, preventing abuse, rate-limiting AI calls: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)), limited to identifiers, counters and technical data, with a right to object.
  • Complying with legal obligations and handling GDPR requests: legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)).
  • Optional tips and offers by email, and website audience measurement and ad tags: consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time.

Pocklune does no advertising profiling based on your budget and no fully automated decision with legal effects.

Data minimisation

Amounts, budgets, expenses, receipts, photos, CSV/OFX/QFX imports and financial notes are stored only on your device in this version. Server data is limited to what is needed to sign in and restore subscriptions.

Pocklune account

You sign in with Google (Android) or Apple (iPhone) through Supabase; Pocklune never receives your password. We receive your account ID, email address (or Apple private relay) and, if provided, display name and profile picture URL. Your profile may also contain the first name, optional last name, country or region and household size you enter, your language, how you heard about Pocklune, reminder preferences, consent choices, and the version and time you accepted the terms and acknowledged this policy.

Tips and offers by email

Optional, unticked by default, recorded with date and version, and withdrawable at any time in the Account section of the app. Your amounts, budgets, receipts or documents are never used to target these messages.

Support form

When signed in, you can send a support request. We store your user ID, first name, last name, subject, message, language, date, status and an expiry date (planned 24 months). Limited to five requests per hour. Do not include passwords, card numbers or financial documents.

Local statement import

A CSV, OFX or QFX file you pick is read and analysed on your device only; nothing from it is sent to us or any third party.

No tracking, no ads in the app

The app contains no ad network, no tracker, no advertising identifier and no third-party analytics tool. We do not sell personal data or track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Website analytics

This public website may load Google Analytics 4, Google Tag Manager and Google Ads measurement tags only after your explicit consent (refused by default). You can change your choice with the “Confidentialité du site” button. No budget or account content is ever sent.

Voice input

Only when you tap “Speak” and allow the microphone. No audio is stored by Pocklune. The recognised phrase is not sent to Supabase, OpenAI, RevenueCat or 2J LAMBRE (the phone’s own speech service may use the network according to its settings).

Premium AI

Available only after sign-in and server-side verification of an active Premium entitlement via RevenueCat, with daily and monthly quotas. The request contains only five bounded category signals: goal, main problem, type of explanation, general situation and language. It never contains amounts, receipts, photos, OCR text, merchants, notes, email or raw user ID. OpenAI receives a hashed, pseudonymous safety identifier. Response storage is disabled (store: false); OpenAI may keep abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days by default.

Purchases

Purchases, trials and subscriptions are handled by the App Store or Google Play, with RevenueCat synchronising entitlements and purchase history. Pocklune never asks for or stores your card number.

Recipients and international transfers

  • Supabase (authentication, profile, consents, support, AI quotas). Standard contractual clauses (SCCs); the project region is not certified here as EU-only.
  • RevenueCat (Pocklune ID, products, purchase receipts, transactions, entitlements, device technical data). Hosted on AWS in the United States; SCCs.
  • OpenAI (category signals and pseudonymous ID for Premium AI). Processing in the United States possible; SCCs or adequacy decision.
  • Google and Apple (sign-in and store purchases, as independent controllers); Google LLC participates in the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.
  • GitHub Pages (website hosting only; IP addresses and technical logs, possibly in the United States).

Retention

  • Local budget data: until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Account, profile and consents: for the life of the account, then deletion on request (legal evidence may be kept separately).
  • Marketing: until consent is withdrawn or the account is deleted.
  • Support: up to 24 months.
  • Purchases: as long as needed for subscriptions, refunds and disputes.
  • AI: answer shown in the session; technical counters kept to enforce limits.
