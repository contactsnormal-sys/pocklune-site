Data controller

2J LAMBRE, SASU, 10 rue de Penthièvre, 75008 Paris, France. Privacy contact and rights requests: contact@2jagency.com (no account needed).

Purposes and legal bases (GDPR)

  • Providing the local budget, account, sign-in and subscription entitlements: performance of contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Answering support requests: performance of contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Sending the deep analysis request (Premium) to OpenAI: consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), asked in the app before the first request. You can withdraw it at any time in Settings › Language and communications › Sharing for deep analysis; no request is sent after that. Declining or withdrawing does not prevent you from using the rest of the app.
  • Securing the service, preventing abuse, rate-limiting deep analysis calls: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)), limited to identifiers, counters and technical data, with a right to object.
  • Complying with legal obligations and handling GDPR requests: legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)).
  • Website audience measurement and ad tags: consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time.

Pocklune does no advertising profiling based on your budget and no fully automated decision with legal effects.

Data minimisation

Amounts, budgets, expenses, receipts, photos, CSV/OFX/QFX imports and financial notes are stored only on your device in this version. Data sent to services is limited to the features you use: optional account and profile, purchases and restoration, support and Premium deep analysis with your consent.

Pocklune account

After the initial questions, you can choose “Continue without an account”: your budget stays stored on your device. A Pocklune account is optional. Plus and Premium offers, purchases and purchase restoration are available without an account.

Without a Pocklune account, RevenueCat assigns an anonymous purchase identifier, managed by its SDK, to recognise subscription entitlements. If you sign in later, the app calls logIn with your account identifier to link purchases under RevenueCat's rules. Signing out of Pocklune preserves local data and does not reset this purchase identity; entitlements remain verified with the purchase service.

The account lets you sync your profile and legal acceptances, use the support form and, with a verified Premium entitlement and your consent, request remote deep analysis. It does not back up your budget to a server in this version. You sign in with Google (Android) or Apple (iPhone) through Supabase; Pocklune never receives your password.

Only if you create or connect an account, we receive your account ID, email address (or Apple private relay) and, if provided, the display name and, for Google, the profile picture link, kept by the authentication service and not used by Pocklune. Your profile may also contain the first name, optional last name, country or region and household size you enter, your language, how you heard about Pocklune, reminder preferences, and the version and time you accepted the terms and acknowledged this policy.

Security, account confirmation, transaction and contractual notices may still require strictly necessary emails; these are not advertising messages.

Support form

When signed in, you can send a support request. We store your user ID, first name, last name, subject, message, language, date, status and an expiry date (planned 24 months). Limited to five requests per hour. Do not include passwords, card numbers or financial documents.

Local statement import

A CSV, OFX or QFX file you pick is read and analysed on your device only; nothing from it is sent to us or any third party.

No tracking, no ads in the app

The app contains no ad network, no tracker, no advertising identifier and no audience measurement or usage analytics tool in the app. The app sends no usage data to a measurement tool. We do not sell personal data or track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Website analytics

This public website may load Google Analytics 4, Google Tag Manager and Google Ads measurement tags only after your explicit consent (refused by default). You can change your choice with the “Confidentialité du site” button. No budget or account content is ever sent.

Voice input

Voice input starts only when you tap “Speak” and allow the microphone. Pocklune neither receives nor retains any audio. The dictated phrase is transcribed by the system speech recognition service: on your device if on-device recognition is supported and the language is installed; otherwise, the Apple or Google service may use the network according to your phone's settings and the provider's policy. Your envelope names are passed to this service as recognition hints.

The recognised phrase becomes an expense suggestion that you review, correct and confirm before saving. Pocklune does not send this phrase to Supabase, OpenAI, RevenueCat or 2J LAMBRE.

Deep analysis (Premium)

Since version 1.0.190, no request is sent to OpenAI without your explicit consent, asked before the first analysis, which you can withdraw in Settings. Available only after sign-in and server-side verification of an active Premium entitlement via RevenueCat, with daily and monthly quotas. The request contains only five bounded category signals: goal, main problem, type of explanation, general situation and language. It never contains amounts, receipts, photos, OCR text, merchants, notes, email or raw user ID. OpenAI receives a hashed, pseudonymous safety identifier. Response storage is disabled (store: false); OpenAI may keep abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days by default. API data is not used to train OpenAI’s models by default.

Purchases

Pocklune offers Free, Plus and Premium (with deep analysis). Purchases, trials and subscriptions are handled by the App Store or Google Play, with RevenueCat synchronising entitlements and purchase history. Pocklune never asks for or stores your card number.

Recipients and international transfers

  • Supabase (authentication, profile, consents, support, deep analysis quotas). Standard contractual clauses (SCCs); the project region is not certified here as EU-only.
  • RevenueCat (SDK-managed anonymous purchase identifier without a Pocklune account, or account identifier after sign-in, products, purchase receipts, transactions, entitlements, device technical data). Hosted on AWS in the United States; SCCs.
  • OpenAI (category signals and pseudonymous ID for Deep analysis, a Premium feature). Under the DPA, OpenAI may process the data in the United States, under the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses (SCCs), or in a country covered by a Commission adequacy decision.
  • Currency converter (Exchange Rate API, open.er-api.com, and the European Central Bank): only if you use the converter, the app requests reference rates; only the requested currency code and the connection’s technical IP address reach them. No account or budget data is sent.
  • Google and Apple (sign-in and store purchases, as independent controllers); Google LLC participates in the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.
  • GitHub Pages (website hosting only; IP addresses and technical logs, possibly in the United States).

Retention

Purchases and subscriptions are managed by the App Store or Google Play and by our payment provider RevenueCat; to request their deletion, email contact@2jagency.com from the account address before deleting the account.

  • Local budget data: until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Account, profile and consents: for the life of the account, then deletion on request (legal evidence may be kept separately).
  • Support: up to 24 months.
  • Purchases: as long as needed for subscriptions, refunds and disputes.
  • Deep analysis: answer shown in the session; technical counters kept to enforce limits.
