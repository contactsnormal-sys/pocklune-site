Frequently asked questions

Do I need to link a bank account?

No. Pocklune does not ask for your bank login and uses no aggregator. You enter your income, bills and spending, or import a statement you choose.

How can I track what is left to spend each day?

Pocklune shows you each day what is left to spend this month and lets you check the calculation. The site's calculator also helps you work out a daily spending guide.

What are budget envelopes?

Envelopes are your budget categories. You assign a budget to each category to organise your spending.

Where is my budget data stored?

Your budget data, receipts and photos stay on your phone. The account is used for sign-in and to manage subscriptions.

Does Pocklune show ads?

No. The Pocklune app does not contain ads.

Where can I see Pocklune's price?

The price is shown on the App Store or Google Play before any payment.
